About us

A place to pause.
A feeling of home.

At Classic Home Brew, we don't just brew coffee, we brew memories. Here on General E Topacio Street in Imus, cozy city comforts meet a peaceful little garden escape.

Settle into warm leather seating, deep navy interiors, and corners filled with greenery. Or step outside to our covered patios and garden seating. From coffee dates and casual catch-ups to work and study sessions, there's a spot to make yourself at home.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know,
before you go.

Planning a coffee date, a study session, or a little garden escape? Here are a few things to help you feel at home.

What is Classic Home Brew?

Classic Home Brew is a café and coffee shop in Imus where coffee, comfort food, and good company come together. Our contemporary interiors and leafy garden seating offer a cozy setting for coffee dates, casual meetups, and everyday moments.

Where exactly are you located?

You’ll find us at General E Topacio St, Imus, Philippines, 4103.

View Classic Home Brew on Google Maps (opens in a new tab) for directions to the café.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, GCash, and credit and debit cards.

What are your café policies and house rules?
  • No outside food.
  • Pets are allowed in the alfresco area, the outdoor part of the café.
Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes. Our outdoor spaces include garden seating and covered patios surrounded by trees and greenery. Take a look around before you visit.
